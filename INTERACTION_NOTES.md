# Kevin Interaction Refinements

The subject workspace now includes a compact **continue studying** rail directly beneath the subject description. A student can enter the grounded Q&A, flashcard review, or quiz flow with one keyboard-accessible action; those options are disabled until source material is available. This shortens the route from subject context to the most common study actions without hiding the more detailed tabs and generation controls.

Material upload recovery also retains a selected PDF when processing fails. It offers a direct retry and a deliberate fallback to pasted text, rather than discarding the student's selection or presenting a toast without a recovery path.
